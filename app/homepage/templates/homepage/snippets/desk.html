{% load i18n static %} {% comment %} The enrolment desk. Naming the branch a child would join means reading the branches' own ages, so it is drawn from the branches rather than from any page content a troop writes. The years are the branches' age ranges read backwards — a child born in year Y is `reference_year - Y` on the troop's age reference day — so a troop that renames or re-ages its branches changes this page without touching it. The branches are drawn as one strip of four rooms: each as wide as its branch is long, in that branch's own colour, in the order the branches are read in. The room is what carries the colour and the words are written in the branch's *ink* — the same hue taken down to where it can be read — so a branch states itself in its own colour instead of in the page's, and Baladins' light blue, which cannot be small text on anything pale, is the reason the two are not the same value. {% endcomment %}
